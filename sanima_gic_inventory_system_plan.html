Reports & audit log

Stock valuation, transfer history, user activity, low-stock alerts

Costing logic

Inbound cost

Recorded at actual purchase price — the exact price on the vendor invoice. Each batch can have a different unit price.

Outbound cost

Issued at weighted average cost — total stock value ÷ total quantity on hand, recalculated after every inbound transaction.

WAC = (Current stock value + New purchase value) ÷ (Current qty + New qty)
